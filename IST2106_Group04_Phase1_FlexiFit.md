### 2. Problem Statement and Target Customer Segments

#### Problem Statement & Significance
In urban Uganda, working adults seeking sustainable health and fitness face three compounded barriers:
1. **Severe Geographic & Time Friction:** An office worker leaving Kololo, Nakawa, or the Kampala CBD at 5:00 PM faces 60–90 minutes of gridlock traffic to reach suburban gyms in Ntinda, Kiwatule, or Kira, making consistent weekday attendance impractical.
2. **Financial Exclusion:** Standard Kampala gym memberships range between UGX 150,000 and UGX 350,000 per month, with dedicated personal trainers charging an additional UGX 20,000 to UGX 50,000 per session (exceeding UGX 500,000/month). Over 80% of entry- and mid-level employees cannot sustain this expense.
3. **The Cultural Nutrition Void:** Fitness success is 70% nutritional adherence. Existing digital fitness apps (MyFitnessPal, Freeletics) exclusively catalog Western diets (quinoa, kale, oats, Greek yogurt). Ugandan professionals whose everyday diets consist of *matooke, posho, sweet potatoes (lumonde), beans, binyebwa (groundnut sauce), and fresh fish* have no tool to calculate macronutrients or calorie intake, leading to frustration and abandoned health regimens.
4. **Trainer Income Instability (Supply Side):** Freelance gym instructors only earn when physically training a client. When evening downpours occur or clients cancel due to workplace overtime, instructors earn zero. Furthermore, gym owners extract 30–50% commission cuts from freelance fees.

#### Target Customer Segments
* **Primary Segment (Demand Side):** Urban Corporate Professionals (Aged 24–42) in Kampala. Sedentary white-collar workers (bankers, ICT professionals, consultants) who value time flexibility, experience gym intimidation, and desire affordable guidance tailored to local foods.
* **Secondary Segment (Supply Side):** Certified Independent Fitness Trainers (Aged 22–35). Qualified trainers seeking to scale their client rosters beyond physical gym floor capacity through digital asynchronous coaching.

### 3. Customer Research Methods & Fieldwork Findings

#### Fieldwork Methodology & Demographics
Group 4 conducted primary fieldwork in Kampala between 19 October and 23 October 2026. Data was gathered through semi-structured interviews and an in-person questionnaire administered to **10 stakeholders**:
* **8 Urban Corporate Professionals** (5 female, 3 male; aged 25–38; employed across Nakawa Business Park, Kololo, and Central Kampala).
* **2 Certified Freelance Gym Instructors** (operating out of commercial fitness centres in Ntinda and Makerere).

#### Key Quantitative Findings
* **100% of professionals (8/8)** reported that evening traffic jams and unpredictable work hours caused them to abandon previous gym routines within 3 months.
* **87.5% (7/8)** had previously downloaded global fitness apps, but uninstalled them within 2–3 weeks because the dietary databases did not recognize Ugandan staple foods.
* **75.0% (6/8)** expressed significant anxiety regarding gym intimidation, feeling uncomfortable in crowded, mirror-lined commercial gyms.
* **100% of trainers (2/2)** confirmed that client no-shows and weather disruptions reduce their projected monthly income by 30–40%, and confirmed high willingness to coach clients remotely if provided an organized tracking tool.

#### Qualitative Voice-of-Customer Quotes

### 5. Preliminary Technology Opportunity & Value Proposition

#### Problem-Technology Fit Architecture
FlexiFit is engineered as a lean, low-bandwidth hybrid system designed for Ugandan mobile constraints:
1. **Client Mobile Application (Low-Data / Offline Caching):** Pre-caches 30-second exercise demonstration clips over Wi-Fi/low-cost data to prevent mobile data drain. Features a **Visual Ugandan Food Portions Guide**, allowing users to log meals using standard household measures (e.g., fists of posho, serving spoons of beans) mapped to scientific nutritional values.
2. **Trainer Web/Mobile Dashboard:** A simplified client-management engine where trainers review weekly client check-ins, assess body weight trend graphs, and assign regional meal templates in minutes.
3. **Automated Asynchronous Accountability Engine:** Integrates automated SMS/WhatsApp workout reminders and flags missed client check-ins for personal trainer follow-up.

#### Core Value Proposition
* **For Urban Clients:** Culturally relevant nutrition guidance and personalized fitness coaching from certified local trainers at **UGX 50,000–70,000 per month** (an 80% cost reduction compared to in-person trainers), completed at home with zero traffic stress.
* **For Fitness Trainers:** Increases coaching capacity from 6 physical clients to 40+ remote clients, tripling baseline monthly earnings while eliminating venue rental cuts.
